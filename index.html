3 Indonesian Heroes Who Can Inspire the Younger Generation

Raden Ajeng Kartini

Raden Ajeng Kartini (R.A. Kartini) is an official female national hero from Indonesia. She is from Java, and she is known for fighting for the education rights of Javanese girls and women. She is also known to be a hard worker, since she worked hard to fight for girls' and women's education rights. Asides from fighting for the education rights of Javanese girls and women, she also established Kartini Schools in Java and in other areas as well.

From this Indonesian hero, the younger generation can learn to never stop fighting for something that should be theirs. R.A. Kartini fought hard for the education rights of Javanese girls and women, and the younger generation can learn to do the same as well, in the same context and in different contexts as well. The younger generation can also learn to be more motivated to study from this national hero, and we can see this by looking at how R.A. Kartini fought non-stop for the education rights of Javanese girls and women, because she believed that girls and women deserved to learn as well. R.A. Kartini can also help empower girls and women, since she proved that whatever boys and men are allowed to do, girls and women should be allowed to as well.

Sutomo

Sutomo (Bung Tomo) is an official male national hero from Indonesia. He played a huge role in the Battle of Surabaya, because of the rousing speeches he gave using the radio. People said that his speeches really boosted the morale of the people of Surabaya when morale was low. He is also most famous for a quote in his speech on the 9th of November 1945, which was "MERDEKA ATAU MATI!" or in English, "FREEDOM OR DEATH!"

From this national hero, the younger generation can learn to use their skills for good. We can see this by looking at how Bung Tomo used his ability to give rousing speeches to motivate and boost the morale of the people of Surabaya. From this national hero, the younger generation can also learn to appreciate Indonesia's freedom and independence, and they can also learn to never stop fighting for it. We can see this by looking at his famous quote, "FREEDOM OR DEATH!" that shows how much freedom is worth, and how much the opposite was basically worse than death.

Cut Nyak Dhien

Cut Nyak Dhien is an official female national hero from Indonesia. She played a huge role in the Aceh War, since she was the leader of the Acehnese guerrilla forces during the Aceh War. She was a very good fighter, and it's amazing how she was, since it wasn't really looked up to, to be a woman and a fighter at her era. It's also amazing how she fought and lead so well, despite both of her husbands dying.

From this national hero, the younger generation, specifically the female side, can learn to exceed society's expectations. We can see this by looking at how Cut Nyak Dhien fought in a war, despite the fact that being a woman and a fighter wasn't normalized at her era. From this hero, the younger generation can also learn to fight for their rights and land. We can see this by looking at how Cut Nyak Dhien fought for Aceh and risked her life for her region.
